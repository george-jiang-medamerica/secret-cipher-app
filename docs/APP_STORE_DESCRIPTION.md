# App Store Marketing Copy

## App Name
Secret Cipher - Create & Share Secret Codes

## Short Description (80 characters)
Create custom ciphers, encode secret messages, and share via QR codes!

## Full Description

**Turn messages into secret codes with Secret Cipher!**

Create your own custom substitution ciphers and encode messages that only your friends can decode. Perfect for kids, families, and anyone who loves puzzles and secret codes!

### KEY FEATURES

**Create Custom Ciphers**
- Design your own unique substitution ciphers with fun icons
- Each cipher uses a randomized character mapping
- Save multiple ciphers for different friend groups

**Encode & Decode Messages**
- Transform plain text into secret coded messages
- Decode messages from friends instantly
- Copy encoded messages to share anywhere

**Share via QR Code**
- Generate QR codes for your custom ciphers
- Friends scan to get your cipher instantly
- No accounts, no sign-ups, no hassle

**100% Private & Offline**
- All data stored locally on your device
- No data collection or tracking
- No internet connection required
- No ads, no subscriptions

### PERFECT FOR

- Kids learning about codes and ciphers
- Families creating secret message games
- Friends sharing private notes
- Teachers introducing cryptography concepts
- Anyone who loves puzzles and secret codes

### HOW IT WORKS

1. Create a custom cipher with your chosen name and icon
2. Encode your secret messages using the cipher
3. Share your cipher via QR code with trusted friends
4. Friends scan the QR code to decode your messages
5. Exchange secret messages back and forth!

### EDUCATIONAL & FUN

Secret Cipher introduces users to the fascinating world of cryptography through hands-on experience with substitution ciphers. While not designed for secure communications, it's a perfect educational tool and entertaining way to send messages to friends.

### PRIVACY FIRST

- No personal information collected
- No user accounts required
- No analytics or tracking
- Camera only used for QR scanning when you choose
- All ciphers stored locally on your device

Start creating secret codes today with Secret Cipher!
