# Privacy Policy for Secret Cipher

**Last Updated:** October 28, 2025

## Introduction

Secret Cipher ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

Secret Cipher is designed with privacy as a core principle:

- **No Data Collection**: We do not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics
- **No User Accounts**: The app does not require or support user accounts
- **No Third-Party Services**: We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services
- **Local Storage Only**: All cipher data is stored exclusively on your device

## Data Storage

All information created or used by Secret Cipher is stored locally on your device:

- **Cipher Configurations**: Custom substitution ciphers you create are stored only on your device
- **Messages**: The app does not store any messages you encode or decode
- **No Cloud Sync**: Data is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party services
- **User Control**: You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the application

## Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

### Camera Permission
- **Purpose**: Required for scanning QR codes to import ciphers shared by others
- **Usage**: The camera is only activated when you explicitly choose to scan a QR code
- **Data Handling**: Images from the camera are processed locally and are not stored or transmitted

### Clipboard Access
- **Purpose**: To facilitate copying encoded messages and pasting them for decoding
- **Usage**: Only accessed when you explicitly use copy/paste functions
- **Data Handling**: Clipboard data is processed locally and not stored by the app

## Data Sharing

**We do not share any data because we do not collect any data.**

When you choose to share a cipher via QR code:
- The QR code is generated locally on your device
- You control who sees the QR code
- We do not have access to or track these sharing activities

## Children's Privacy

Secret Cipher does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is designed to be safe for users of all ages as it operates entirely offline with no data collection.

## Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- The security of your ciphers depends on your device's security
- We recommend using device-level security features (passcodes, biometric authentication)
- Be cautious about who you share your cipher QR codes with, as anyone with access can decode your messages

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Educational and Entertainment Use

Secret Cipher is designed for educational and entertainment purposes. The substitution ciphers provided are not cryptographically secure and should not be used for protecting sensitive information.
